# Where can I find best practices and resources for meeting the CRA's requirements?

ORC WG's [inventory of resources][inventory] collects the industry and community best practices that bear on the CRA's requirements for open source, by topic: secure design and development principles, vulnerability management, with specifications, policy templates and the policies of existing foundations, SBOMs, with the specifications, software identification and implementation guidelines, due diligence, security attestations, and similar legislation elsewhere. Each section says which requirement of the CRA it serves. The inventory is a working group document, kept up to date as resources appear, and anyone can add to it by pull request. The working group also keeps a list of [talks and articles][community resources] on the CRA.

The official material comes first: the Commission's guidance and FAQ, ENISA's technical advisories, which are published under a Creative Commons licence, and, once published, the harmonised standards ([[cra-itself/guidance]], [[standards/harmonised-standards]]). Which practices a project should adopt first, and why: [[projects/practices-to-adopt]]. What documentation helps the manufacturers that build on a project: [[due-diligence/support]].

If you know of a resource the inventory lacks, or your ecosystem publishes one, add it there by pull request.

[inventory]: https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/inventory.md
[community resources]: https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/community-resources.md
