# What happens if a project under a steward becomes inactive (all contributors leave)?

If a project under an open-source software steward becomes inactive because all its contributors have left, the steward's obligations under the CRA for that project end once the steward stops supporting it. The Commission's guidance says that where an organisation _"ceases to provide systematic support on a sustained basis for a specific FOSS, it may no longer meet the definition of steward and may therefore no longer be subject to the corresponding obligations"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]).

This follows from the definition: a steward is a legal person _"systematically providing support on a sustained basis"_ for a project and _"ensures the viability"_ of it ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]). Once you archive the project or remove it from your stewardship, you no longer meet that definition for it. The guidance encourages you to _"communicate clearly the change in its status"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]): say so where the project's users will see it.

Even if you had fallen short of your obligations while the project was active, no administrative fines apply to stewards ([[CRA Article 64(10)(b)]]); a market surveillance authority can still require corrective action ([[CRA Article 52(3)]]).

For more on steward obligations and what happens when they aren't met, see [[stewards/obligations]] and [[stewards/penalties]].
