# What happens if a project under a steward becomes inactive (all contributors leave)?

If a project you steward becomes inactive because all its contributors have left, your obligations as its _open-source software steward_ end once you stop supporting it. The Commission's guidance says that where an organisation _"ceases to provide systematic support on a sustained basis for a specific FOSS, it may no longer meet the definition of steward and may therefore no longer be subject to the corresponding obligations"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]).

This follows from the definition: a steward is a company or foundation _"systematically providing support on a sustained basis"_ for a project and _"ensures the viability"_ of it ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]). Once you archive the project or remove it from your stewardship, you no longer meet that definition for it. The guidance encourages you to _"communicate clearly the change in its status"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]): say so where the project's users will see it.

For the obligations themselves, see [[stewards/obligations]]; for what happens if you fell short of them while the project was active, see [[stewards/penalties]].
