# What happens when an _open-source software steward_ doesn't meet its obligations?

A steward that doesn't meet its obligations can't be fined, but a market surveillance authority can still require it to put things right. The Commission's Q&A gives the general rule: in case of non-compliance, authorities _"could require operators to bring the non-compliance to an end"_ ([[cra-basics/what-will-happen-to-non-compliant-products]]). For stewards, the CRA takes the fine away: its administrative fines do not apply to _"any infringement of this Regulation by open-source software stewards"_ ([[CRA Article 64(10)(b)]]). The Commission's guidance says nothing more specific about stewards.

What an authority can do is order corrective action. Where it finds that a steward does not comply with its obligations under [[CRA Article 24]], it _"shall require the open-source software steward to ensure that all appropriate corrective actions are taken"_ ([[CRA Article 52(3)]]).

Other kinds of fines are not an option either: the CRA tells Member States not to _"impose other kinds of penalties with pecuniary character"_ on stewards ([[CRA Recital 120]]).

For the obligations themselves, see [[stewards/obligations]]; for what enforcement is likely to look like in practice, see [[stewards/enforcement]].
