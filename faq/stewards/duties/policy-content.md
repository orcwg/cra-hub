# What must a steward's cybersecurity policy contain?

The policy has to say how the project develops secure software and how its developers handle vulnerabilities. [[CRA Article 24(1)]] sets its content in four points. It fosters the development of a secure product and the effective handling of vulnerabilities by the project's developers. It covers, in particular, documenting, addressing and remediating vulnerabilities. It fosters the voluntary reporting of vulnerabilities to a CSIRT or ENISA under [[CRA Article 15]]. And it promotes the sharing of information about discovered vulnerabilities within the open source community. Neither the Commission's FAQ nor its guidance says more about what goes in it.

The policy must be documented _"in a verifiable manner"_ and take into account _"the specific nature of the open-source software steward and the legal and organisational arrangements to which it is subject"_ ([[CRA Article 24(1)]]). A foundation that only sets governance rules therefore writes a different policy from one that employs the maintainers. The Commission's guidance says a steward that gives only non-technical support still passes on the vulnerability reports it receives to the maintainers _"in accordance with its cybersecurity policy"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 80]]).

ORC WG's [white paper on stewards and the CRA][white paper] works through what such a policy looks like in its section on the security policy. It calls for processes for documenting vulnerabilities once fixed, triaging and prioritising reports, remediating, sharing information, and letting the manufacturers that use the project report to it, together with evidence that the policy is followed. The practices a policy can ask a project to adopt are those in [[projects/practices-to-adopt]].

The policy applies to how the project itself is developed, not to how others integrate it: see [[stewards/policy-scope]]. It can be written in one language: see [[stewards/translation-costs]]. For the steward's other obligations, see [[stewards/obligations]].
