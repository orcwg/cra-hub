# How do stewards outside the EU select which CSIRT to report to?

If you are a steward outside the EU, you select your CSIRT by the same rule as manufacturers, which for most stewards means the CSIRT of the EU country where your software has the most users. ENISA says that _"manufacturers and, once applicable, open-source software stewards are responsible for identifying the correct CSIRT designated as coordinator (CDaC) in accordance with Art. 14(7) of the CRA"_ ([[srp/faq_18]]).

That rule points to the EU country of your _main establishment_, where decisions about your software's security are mostly taken, and otherwise to the country of your authorised representative, importer or distributor, or _"if none of the above applies, the Member State with the highest number of users"_ ([[CRA Article 14(7)]], [[srp/faq_18]]). As a steward you normally have none of the first three, so the last step applies; once you have reported to that CSIRT, you may keep reporting to it ([[CRA Article 14(7)]]).

Neither ENISA nor the Commission's guidance says what to do if you cannot tell where your users are: see [[pending-guidance/csirt-identification]]. What a CSIRT is: see [[vulnerability-handling/csirt]]. What stewards have to report: see [[stewards/notification-obligations]].
