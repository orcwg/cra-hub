# Do a steward's reporting obligations cover projects that predate the CRA?

Yes. The Commission's FAQ says the reporting obligations _"apply to open-source software stewards under certain circumstances"_ ([[official/faq_5-5]], [[CRA Article 24(3)]]). Those circumstances are about the steward's involvement in the project, not the project's age. A project started before the CRA is covered like any other once the obligations apply.

The support period does not come into it. A steward has none: its obligations last as long as it sustains the project and end when it stops. See [[stewards/inactive]].

For when the steward obligations start, see [[stewards/start-date]]; for what a steward reports and to whom, [[stewards/notification-obligations]].
