# What does 'objective of systematically providing support on a sustained basis' mean?

Systematically providing support on a sustained basis means an organisation has made supporting a specific open source project part of its purpose, and does so continuously rather than now and then. The phrase is part of the steward definition in [[CRA Article 3(14)]], which also asks that the organisation _"ensures the viability"_ of the project.

The Commission's guidance says this support can take many forms. Quoting [[CRA Recital 19]], it lists _"(i) the hosting and managing of software development collaboration platforms; (ii) hosting source code or software; (iii) governing or managing products with digital elements qualifying as FOSS; and (iv) steering the development of such products"_, a list that is not exhaustive ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 75]]). It also distinguishes non-technical support, such as governance rules or collecting donations, from providing the project's IT infrastructure and from contributing engineering resources ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 79]]). All three make an organisation a steward; the kind of support decides which reporting obligations apply, see [[stewards/notification-obligations]].

What separates a steward from an organisation that is merely involved is that the support is systematic, sustained and aimed at a specific project. A foundation that hosts many projects is a steward only for those it systematically supports and keeps viable ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]]), and an organisation that stops providing that support may no longer be a steward for that project ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]).

For the rest of the definition, see [[stewards/what-is-a-steward]].
