# Is stewardship something you opt in to, or can you be designated as a steward, and if so, by whom?

Neither. Your organisation is an _open-source software steward_ when it meets the definition. What counts is what it does, not whether it calls itself a steward or someone names it one. The definition has four parts: a company or foundation (a _legal person_); other than a manufacturer; whose purpose is to systematically provide sustained support for specific open source software intended for commercial use; and which ensures that software's viability ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]). An organisation that fits it _"is subject to the obligations of stewards"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]]).

Nobody designates stewards. The CRA gives no authority, project or other organisation the power to appoint one. Regulators will not nominate a steward for a project that has none: see [[stewards/nominations]]. A project calling a foundation its steward doesn't make it one. A foundation hosting a project isn't its steward either, unless it gives that project systematic support and ensures its viability ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]]).

There is no opting out either. For each project you publish, you have to work out which role you have. If you place it on the market, you are its manufacturer. If you sustain it without placing it on the market, you are its steward ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]). What counts as sustained support: see [[stewards/sustained-support]]. The role only reaches organisations; an individual can't be a steward, whatever support they give: see [[maintainers/solo-maintainer-steward]].

The part of the definition an organisation decides for itself is its purpose: it has to _"have the purpose or objective"_ of systematically sustaining the project ([[CRA Article 3(14)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]]). A foundation that takes a project in and commits to sustaining it, or a company that keeps one alive because its products depend on it, has made that its purpose. That is as close as the CRA comes to opting in: a decision to sustain the project, shown by what the organisation then does, not a declaration. An organisation that merely hosts a project, or helps it now and then, has not ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]]; see [[stewards/sustained-support]]).

So the choices you have are whether to take on the sustained support of a project, which is that purpose, and whether to monetise it. For what being a steward requires, see [[stewards/obligations]].
