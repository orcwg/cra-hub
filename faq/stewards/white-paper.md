# Where can I read more about what the CRA asks of stewards?

ORC WG's [white paper on open-source software stewards and the CRA][white paper] is the longer treatment of the steward role, written by the working group's Cyber Resilience SIG. It is also available as a [PDF][] and as [Markdown on GitHub][markdown].

The paper walks through each obligation of [[CRA Article 24]] in turn: the security policy, with what a policy that develops secure software and handles vulnerabilities effectively has to cover; collaboration with market surveillance authorities; mandatory reporting of exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents; informing users; and voluntary reporting. For each it says what the steward and the project will need, and which resources the community could create, such as a policy template and ways to show the policy is followed.

The paper is a community document under a CC-BY licence, not an official source. Where the Commission's FAQ or guidance has since settled a point, the FAQs here follow them. Start with [[stewards/obligations]] and [[stewards/policy-content]].
