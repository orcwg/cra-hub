# Does an open source steward outside the EU need an authorised representative in the EU?

No, an open source steward based outside the EU does not need an authorised representative in the EU. The CRA never asks stewards to appoint one.

An _authorised representative_ is a person or company established in the EU that a _manufacturer_ has mandated in writing to act on its behalf ([[CRA Article 3(15)]]). Only a manufacturer can appoint one, and even a manufacturer only _"may"_ do so ([[CRA Article 18(1)]]). The tasks a mandate covers, keeping the technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity, handing them to market surveillance authorities and cooperating with those authorities, are manufacturers' tasks ([[CRA Article 18(3)]]); a steward has none of them.

What a steward has to do is listed in [[CRA Article 24]]: put in place and document a cybersecurity policy, cooperate with market surveillance authorities when they ask, and, depending on how involved it is in a project's development, report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents ([[CRA Article 24(3)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 79]]). None of this depends on where the steward is based. The definition of a steward requires a _legal person_ (a company or a foundation) but says nothing about where it is established ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]), and neither the regulation nor the Commission's guidance on stewards ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Section 3.3]]) mentions an authorised representative or any other presence in the EU.

A foundation in the United States or a company in Switzerland that stewards a project therefore deals with the EU directly: it answers a reasoned request from a market surveillance authority itself, in a language the authority can easily understand ([[CRA Article 24(2)]]), and, where it has to report, it reports itself through ENISA's single reporting platform ([[CRA Article 14(1)]], [[srp/faq_1]]). Which national CSIRT a steward with no establishment in the EU reports to: see [[stewards/which-csirt]]. Which stewards have to report at all: see [[stewards/notification-obligations]].
