# Can a project choose its steward?

No, a project cannot choose its steward, and an organisation cannot opt in to the role or be designated for it either: an organisation is the steward of a project when what it does for that project meets the CRA's definition, and not otherwise.

Neither the Commission's FAQ nor its guidance addresses a project appointing a steward, or an organisation registering as one, and the CRA has no procedure for either. What the guidance does say is that the role follows from the facts. An organisation that publishes software without selling it and _"has the purpose or objective of systematically providing support on a sustained basis"_ for it _"is subject to the obligations of stewards"_ ([[CRA Article 3(14)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]]), and _"for each specific FOSS that it publishes, the legal person will need to ascertain"_ whether it is selling it, stewarding it or neither ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]). The hub's reading is that this leaves no room for choosing: a statement in a project's documentation, or in an organisation's, changes nothing on its own.

What a project and an organisation can do is change the facts. An organisation becomes a steward by taking on the role in practice: making it its purpose to sustain the project and doing so, for instance by hosting the code or the collaboration platform, governing or steering the project, or employing its developers ([[CRA Recital 19]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 75]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 79]]). A project that wants a steward can therefore move under a foundation or a company that will sustain it in that way, and an organisation that wants to be one can set itself up to do so: see [[stewards/foundation]]. The reverse holds too: _"Where an entity ceases to provide systematic support on a sustained basis for a specific FOSS, it may no longer meet the definition of steward"_, and the guidance encourages it to say so clearly ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 83]]).

No organisation can be made a steward against the facts. A company that only contributes code or money to a project someone else publishes and controls is not its steward, however much the project might like it to be: see [[stewards/support-without-control]]. An individual cannot be one at all, since a steward is a legal person: see [[maintainers/solo-maintainer-steward]].

For what the relationship between a project and its steward looks like, see [[stewards/relationship]]. For whether a project can have more than one steward, see [[stewards/multiple]]. For whether regulators will nominate stewards for important projects that have none, see [[stewards/nominations]].
