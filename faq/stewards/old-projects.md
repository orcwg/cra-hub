# Do a steward's reporting obligations cover projects that predate the CRA?

Yes. The Commission's FAQ says the reporting obligations of Article 14(1), (3) and (8) _"apply to open-source software stewards under certain circumstances"_, from 11 December 2027 ([[official/faq_5-5]], [[CRA Article 24(3)]]). Nothing limits them to projects started after the CRA. From that date, a steward reports actively exploited vulnerabilities in the projects it is involved in developing, and severe incidents affecting the infrastructure it provides for them, whatever the age of the project.

The CRA says the same for manufacturers. Reporting applies to every product in scope, including products placed on the market before 11 December 2027 ([[CRA Article 69(3)]], [[official/faq_5-3]]). It is the one obligation the CRA extends to old products.

The support period does not come into it. A steward has none: its obligations last as long as it sustains the project and end when it stops. See [[stewards/inactive]].

For what a steward reports and to whom, see [[stewards/notification-obligations]] and [[stewards/which-csirt]].
