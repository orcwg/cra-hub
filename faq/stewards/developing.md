# If a steward also develops the software, doesn't that make it a manufacturer?

No. Developing the software does not make a steward its manufacturer; placing it on the market does. The Commission's guidance says a manufacturer _"by definition, is the natural or legal person who places products with digital elements on the market ('markets them')"_, which is why the steward definition says _"other than a manufacturer"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 71]], [[CRA Article 3(14)]]). The manufacturer definition does say _"develops"_ and _"markets"_ ([[CRA Article 3(13)]]). But it is the manufacturer definition of all EU product law, which the Blue Guide states as the person who _"places it on the market under his own name or trademark"_ (section 3.1); _markets them_ is that placing on the market, which the CRA defines as supply in the course of a commercial activity ([[CRA Article 3(21)]], [[CRA Article 3(22)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]]). Advertising a project or putting your name on it is not marketing in this sense.

An organisation that develops open source software therefore shows it is not the manufacturer by showing it does not monetise that software. The guidance gives the procedure: for each project it publishes, the organisation works out whether the software is placed on the market. If so, it is the manufacturer; if not, it may be the steward, provided the software is meant for commercial use and the organisation sustains it ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]). What counts as monetising is in [[cra-itself/monetisation]].

The words _"free of charge"_ in the definition do not change this. Making available on the market is always supply in the course of a commercial activity, paid or not: see [[cra-itself/placing-on-the-market]]. The words cover software without a price but monetised another way, such as a free product through which paid services are sold ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 54]]). A non-profit that charges for its software stays a steward if its earnings after costs serve its non-profit objectives ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 77]]).

For the two roles side by side, see [[stewards/manufacturer-vs-steward]].
