# Do stewards have to register anywhere?

No. Becoming a steward involves no registration, declaration or listing. The steward obligations are three: a cybersecurity policy, cooperation with market surveillance authorities at their request, and reporting in certain circumstances ([[CRA Article 24]]). None of them asks you to tell anyone that you are a steward. Nobody designates stewards either: see [[stewards/opt-in]].

The one registration is on the single reporting platform, and only when you have something to report. ENISA advises manufacturers and stewards to _"register and initiate the validation process only when they need to submit a notification"_ ([[srp/faq_9]]). Registration itself takes a few minutes, but it needs an [EU Login][] account with multi-factor authentication for each person who will report, and getting one can take a while. Create those accounts now, so that nobody is setting one up during the 24 hours you have to report. What a steward does on the platform is submit timely notifications to the extent it is involved with the products concerned ([[srp/faq_19]]). The reporting obligation applies to stewards from 11 December 2027: see [[stewards/start-date]].

For what to report and to whom, see [[stewards/notification-obligations]] and [[stewards/which-csirt]].

[EU Login]: https://ecas.ec.europa.eu/cas/login
