# Is my company the steward of the open source software it publishes?

Yes, if your company publishes the software without selling it, means it for commercial use, and supports it on a sustained basis. Those are the parts of the definition: an _open-source software steward_ is a company or foundation, other than a manufacturer, whose purpose is to systematically provide sustained support for the development of specific open source software intended for commercial activities, and which ensures that software's viability ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]). The Commission's guidance says a company that develops open source software for its own products and publishes it without placing it on the market _"is not a manufacturer of the software, but may be a steward to it"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]]), and that a company which fits the definition _"is subject to the obligations of stewards"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]]).

Each part in turn. Not selling it means not placing it on the market: the software is free to download and use, and your company does not monetise it ([[maintainers/become-manufacturer]]). If it sells a version, it is that version's manufacturer and the steward of the free one ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]]; see [[manufacturers/both-manufacturer-and-steward]]). Intended for commercial use covers software your company builds into its own products or expects other companies to ([[stewards/business-context]]). Sustained support is what a company does when it publishes, hosts, governs or steers the project ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 75]]; see [[stewards/sustained-support]]); code published and abandoned is not sustained. Nothing in the definition requires the software to be someone else's ([[stewards/whose-codebase]]), and developing it yourself does not make you its manufacturer ([[stewards/developing]]).

The role is decided project by project ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]; see [[stewards/relationship]]). Where it applies, it brings the obligations of [[CRA Article 24]] from 11 December 2027 ([[stewards/obligations]], [[stewards/start-date]]). A steward cannot be fined ([[stewards/penalties]]), but the products your company sells with that software in them have to comply as a whole ([[manufacturers/mistakes]]).
