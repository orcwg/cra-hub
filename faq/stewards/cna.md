# Does being a CNA (CVE Numbering Authority) for an open source project make you its steward?

No, being a CNA for an open source project does not, by itself, make you its _open-source software steward_. Neither the CRA nor the Commission's guidance mentions CNAs: whether you are a steward depends only on whether you meet the definition in [[CRA Article 3(14)]].

That definition has several parts, and you have to meet all of them: you are a company, foundation or other legal person, and not the project's manufacturer; your purpose or objective is to _"systematically provid[e] support on a sustained basis"_ for the project's development; the project is free and open-source software that is _"intended for commercial activities"_, such as integration into products that others sell; and you ensure the project's viability ([[CRA Article 3(14)]], [[CRA Recital 19]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 71]]). The Commission's guidance adds that the steward is the legal person that publishes the project without selling it, and that each company or foundation has to work out for itself, project by project, whether it meets the definition ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]). Who fits: [[stewards/who-can-be-steward]].

Assigning CVE identifiers is a narrow service. A CNA does it for vulnerabilities in a project, whether the CNA is a third party, a company involved in the project or the project itself, and nothing in that role means the CNA publishes the project, supports its development or keeps it going. The guidance does list _"handling vulnerability reports and security patches"_ among the ways a steward that contributes engineering resources to a project supports it ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 79]]), so a company that is the CNA for a project it also publishes and sustains will usually be its steward; but it is the publishing and the sustained support that make it one, not the CNA role.

Most open source projects have no steward at all, CNA or not: see [[stewards/do-all-projects-have-stewards]].
