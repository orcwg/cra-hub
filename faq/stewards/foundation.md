# Can a manufacturer set up a foundation that acts as an open-source software steward for community-led projects?

Yes, a manufacturer can set up a foundation that acts as an _open-source software steward_ for community-led projects, but it does not need one: a manufacturer can be a steward itself.

Foundations are among the organisations the CRA has in mind for the steward role: _"Open-source software stewards include certain foundations as well as entities that develop and publish free and open-source software in a business context, including not-for-profit entities"_ ([[CRA Recital 19]]). The Commission's guidance describes foundations that _"offer collaboration platforms with various forms of governance that enable manufacturers to contribute regularly to the development of FOSS and/or that are regularly financed by manufacturers"_, and says they are the stewards of the specific projects they give sustained support to and that are intended for commercial use ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 28]]). Who wrote the code or paid for it does not matter: see [[stewards/whose-codebase]].

A separate foundation is not required, because a manufacturer can be a steward too. The roles are decided per piece of software: a company is the manufacturer of the software it sells, and can at the same time be the steward of open source software it publishes without selling, including the free "community" version of the very product it sells ([[CRA Article 3(14)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 73]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]]). See [[manufacturers/both-manufacturer-and-steward]].

What a foundation cannot do is take the manufacturer's obligations off the manufacturer. Whoever sells or otherwise monetises a product is its manufacturer, whatever other organisation supports the project it is built from ([[CRA Article 3(13)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]): see [[stewards/avoid-manufacturer-obligations]]. The foundation is the steward only of what it actually publishes and supports on a sustained basis ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 70]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]]). Neither the CRA nor the guidance says anything about who controls the foundation; a foundation financed by manufacturers is explicitly among those that can be stewards ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 78]]).
