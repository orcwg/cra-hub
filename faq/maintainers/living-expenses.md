# Can a natural person's living expenses count as 'costs' or is that profit?

Yes. Reasonable living expenses count as costs, not profit. An individual (a natural person) who publishes open source software and charges for technical support, receives donations or the like to cover their costs, including reasonable living expenses, is not considered to be monetising that software on that basis alone, and so is not in scope of the CRA ([[CRA Recital 15]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 58]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 61]]).

On paid support, see [[maintainers/technical-support]]; on donations, see [[maintainers/donations]]. For more on what counts as costs under the CRA, see [[cra-itself/actual-costs]]. For what constitutes profit, see [[cra-itself/profit]].
