# Can a _manufacturer_ also be an _open-source software steward_?

Yes. A company can be the manufacturer of one product and the steward of another, including a free version of the same software. The Commission's guidance says a company _"may therefore simultaneously be a steward for one specific FOSS … and a manufacturer for another specific FOSS"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 73]]). The roles are worked out project by project: for each open source project it publishes, the company asks whether that project is placed on the market, which makes it the manufacturer, and if not, whether it is the project's steward ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 74]]; see [[stewards/relationship]]).

The same holds for two versions of one project. A company that publishes a free community version and sells a monetised version _"is deemed a steward to the free (or community) version, and a manufacturer to the monetised version"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 53]]). It owes its customers a manufacturer's obligations for the version it sells ([[CRA Article 13]]) and the users of the free version a steward's ([[CRA Article 24]]). A company that writes open source software for its own products and publishes it without selling it is likewise not that software's manufacturer, but may be its steward ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]]).

A product under the CRA is each individual copy supplied, not the software as such. The Blue Guide says that _"the concept of making available refers to each individual product"_ (section 2.2), and the Commission's guidance says that a monetised version and a free version of the same code _"are different products"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 52]]). So the two roles never meet on one product. The copies a company sells are products, and the company is their manufacturer. The same code published for free is not placed on the market, and the company may be its steward. A steward is _"other than a manufacturer"_ for the software it stewards ([[CRA Article 3(14)]]), never for what the company sells.

For the product concept, see [[legislation/nlf]] and [[cra-itself/placing-on-the-market]]; for selling support around a free project, [[manufacturers/paid-support]]; for the difference the two roles make, [[stewards/manufacturer-vs-steward]].
