# Are free app store listings considered 'making available on the market' under the CRA?

Yes, a free app store listing is _making available on the market_ if you monetise the app; a free listing of an open source app you don't monetise is not. The Commission's guidance says a mobile app downloaded from an app store is a product with digital elements, and _"where it is placed on the market in the course of a commercial activity, it may fall within the scope of the CRA"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 3]]). What counts is the commercial activity, not the price: the definition covers supply _"whether in return for payment or free of charge"_ ([[CRA Article 3(22)]]).

The guidance says an app that is _"available free of charge"_ is still placed on the market where you use it to monetise other products or services, or require users' personal data as a condition for use for reasons other than security, compatibility or interoperability ([[CRA Recital 15]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 54]]). Its examples:

- a marketplace app earning advertising, commission or subscription fees ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 14]]);
- a VPN app selling extra servers or dedicated IP addresses ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 15]]);
- a fitness app whose use requires processing personal data for targeted advertising or analytics ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 16]]).

Charging for the app itself is a sale, and makes you its manufacturer ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 51]]).

If you don't monetise an open source app at all, listing it on an app store does not bring it within scope: open source software _"not monetised by their manufacturers should not be considered to be a commercial activity"_ ([[CRA Recital 18]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 42]]). The store is only how the app reaches users.

For the forms monetisation can take, see [[cra-itself/monetisation]] and [[maintainers/monetization]].
