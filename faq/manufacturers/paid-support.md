# If you sell support for your open source project, who do your manufacturer obligations cover?

Nobody, as long as the project itself stays free. Selling support, consulting or vulnerability management around open source software that anyone can download, install and update without paying is a service, and the software is not placed on the market: the Commission's guidance says the decisive factor is _"whether access to the FOSS itself … including its maintenance, is conditioned on remuneration"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 56]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 55]]). Your customers get a service, and the software has no manufacturer ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 59]]; see [[maintainers/technical-support]]). Whether your company is the software's steward is a separate question, decided by whether it publishes and sustains the project, not by the service it sells ([[stewards/own-software]]).

Once a version, its updates or its security fixes are reserved for paying customers, that version is a product you place on the market, and you are its manufacturer ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 57]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 17]]). Your obligations then run to everyone you supply that version to, and they include security updates _"free of charge"_ for its support period ([[CRA Annex I, Part II]]). A contract cannot change that for a product sold to many customers; the CRA allows other terms only for a _tailor-made_ product, built for one business user under explicitly agreed terms, and a product that merely gets minor customisation per customer is not tailor-made ([[official/faq_4-2-5]], [[CRA Recital 64]]).

The free version stays a separate product, not placed on the market, and your manufacturer obligations do not reach its users ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 52]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]]). So a company cannot charge for the security updates of a product it has sold, but it can sell a supported version alongside a free one: its customers' security updates are part of what they bought, and the free version's users get what the project gives them. See [[manufacturers/both-manufacturer-and-steward]] and [[maintainers/become-manufacturer]].
