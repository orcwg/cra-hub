# What is a _manufacturer_?

The term _Manufacturer_ is defined in [[CRA Article 3(13)]] of the CRA:

> ‘manufacturer’ means a natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements or has products with digital elements designed, developed or manufactured, and markets them under its name or trademark, whether for payment, monetisation or free of charge;

In this definition, _markets them_ means places them on the market: supplies them in the EU, under your name or trademark, in the course of a commercial activity. The CRA does not define "markets" itself. It defines _placing on the market_ and _making available on the market_ ([[CRA Article 3(21)]], [[CRA Article 3(22)]]), and the Commission's guidance reads the manufacturer definition through them: the manufacturer is the person that supplies a product under its name or trademark _"in the course of a commercial activity (thereby placing it on the market)"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 71]]). That is the manufacturer of EU product law in general. The Blue Guide's manufacturer _"places it on the market under his own name or trademark"_ (section 3.1).

So developing software and putting your name on it does not make you its manufacturer. Supplying it in the course of a commercial activity does, which for open source software means monetising it. See [[cra-itself/placing-on-the-market]], [[cra-itself/monetisation]] and [[stewards/developing]].
