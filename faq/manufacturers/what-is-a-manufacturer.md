# What is a _manufacturer_?

The term _Manufacturer_ is defined in [[CRA Article 3(13)]] of the CRA:

> ‘manufacturer’ means a natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements or has products with digital elements designed, developed or manufactured, and markets them under its name or trademark, whether for payment, monetisation or free of charge;

_Markets them_ means places them on the market: the Commission's guidance defines the manufacturer as the person that supplies a product under its name or trademark _"in the course of a commercial activity (thereby placing it on the market)"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 71]]). Developing software and putting your name on it does not make you its manufacturer; monetising it does. See [[cra-itself/placing-on-the-market]] and [[stewards/developing]].
