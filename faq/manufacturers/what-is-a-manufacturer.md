# What is a _manufacturer_?

The term _Manufacturer_ is defined in [[CRA Article 3(13)]] of the CRA:

> ‘manufacturer’ means a natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements or has products with digital elements designed, developed or manufactured, and markets them under its name or trademark, whether for payment, monetisation or free of charge;

_Markets them_ means places them on the market. "Markets" is not one of the CRA's defined terms; _placing on the market_ and _making available on the market_ are ([[CRA Article 3(21)]], [[CRA Article 3(22)]]). The definition is EU product law's: the Blue Guide's manufacturer is the person who _"manufactures a product or has a product designed or manufactured, and places it on the market under his own name or trademark"_ (section 3.1), and the Commission's guidance reads the CRA's the same way, as the person that supplies a product under its name _"in the course of a commercial activity (thereby placing it on the market)"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 71]]). Developing software and putting your name on it does not make you its manufacturer; monetising it does. See [[cra-itself/placing-on-the-market]] and [[stewards/developing]].
