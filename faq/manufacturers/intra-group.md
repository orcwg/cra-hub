# Does selling software between companies of the same group fall under the CRA?

Yes. Two subsidiaries of the same group are two separate legal persons, and a sale from one to the other is a supply in the course of a commercial activity, which places the software on the market. The selling company is its manufacturer.

The CRA applies to products with digital elements made available on the market, that is, _"supplied for distribution or use on the Union market in the course of a commercial activity, whether in return for payment or free of charge"_ ([[CRA Article 3(22)]], [[official/faq_1-1]]). The Blue Guide, which the Commission's guidance follows for these concepts ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 11]]), says placing on the market _"requires an offer or an agreement (written or verbal) between two or more legal or natural persons for the transfer of ownership, possession or any other property right concerning the product"_ (section 2.3). A contract between two companies is such an agreement, whoever owns them. Neither the CRA, its guidance nor the Commission's FAQ makes an exception for companies of the same group.

Software a company develops for its own use is not placed on the market ([[official/faq_1-5]]), but that exception applies only while the same company builds and uses it. Selling the software to a sister company would not qualify.

If instead the developing company publishes the software as open source, available to everyone, and its sister company takes it from there like anyone else, nothing is sold and neither company is its manufacturer. The publishing company is then its _open-source software steward_, as a company publishing open source software intended for commercial use ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 76]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 29]]), and the sister company that builds it into products it sells owes due diligence on it ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 88]]). Offering paid support around the open source version does not change that, unless access to the software or its updates is conditional on paying: see [[maintainers/technical-support]].
