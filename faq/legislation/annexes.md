# Are the annexes to the CRA legally binding?

Yes. The annexes are part of the regulation and bind in the same way as its articles. The articles do not restate the requirements they impose; they point to the annexes for them. Manufacturers have to design their products _"in accordance with the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Part I of Annex I"_ ([[CRA Article 13(1)]]); the lists of important and critical products are [[CRA Annex III]] and [[CRA Annex IV]] ([[CRA Article 7]], [[CRA Article 8]]); the technical documentation is what [[CRA Annex VII]] lists ([[CRA Article 31]]); and the conformity assessment checks the requirements of Annex I through the procedures of [[CRA Annex VIII]] ([[CRA Article 32]]). Everything a manufacturer or steward has to do under the CRA runs through an article that makes an annex apply.

The recitals, the numbered paragraphs before Article 1, are different. They explain why the regulation says what it says and help to read it, but they create no obligations of their own: an obligation always rests on an article, together with the annex it refers to. The Commission's guidance and its FAQ cite recitals the same way, as explanation next to the article that binds.
