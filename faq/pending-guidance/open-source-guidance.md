# Open source guidance

## Guidance needed

Clarification of when free and open-source software counts as supplied in the course of a commercial activity, and so as placed on the market, and of the roles of manufacturers, stewards and contributors.

## Guidance received

The European Commission published its [guidance on the application of the CRA][official guidance] on 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252 final). [[CRA-GUIDANCE Section 3]] covers free and open-source software: what qualifies as such, when it is under someone's responsibility, when it is placed on the market (charging a price, monetising other services or personal data, support services, donations, financing, not-for-profit entities, integration by other manufacturers), the obligations of stewards, and the position of contributors. The FAQs that reference this request have been updated to cite it.

## Background

As described in [[CRA Article 9]], the CRA Expert Group (see [[cra-itself/cra-expert-group]]) was consulted by the Commission as it prepared the guidance, and had access to draft versions. Until publication, the FAQs that reference this request relied on a conservative reading of the draft.
