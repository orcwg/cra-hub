# Does the CRA regulate open source projects, or the people and organisations behind them?

The CRA regulates people and organisations, not projects. Its obligations fall on the _natural or legal persons_ who place a product on the EU market, the manufacturers, importers and distributors, and on the companies and foundations that are _open-source software stewards_. The Commission's guidance says the CRA _"places key responsibilities on economic operators that supply products with digital elements in the EU market"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 47]]), and that whether it applies to open source software _"depends solely on whether the natural or legal person that publishes it places it on the market"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 87]]).

A project is only the software. What the CRA looks at is who is responsible for it, the people who publish it and control its development and releases ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 49]]; see [[cra-itself/under-responsibility]]), and what they do with it: whether they monetise it ([[CRA Recital 18]]). The answer differs from project to project:

- A project an individual publishes without monetising it: nobody is regulated, and the individual has no obligations ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 53]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 27]]).
- A project a foundation publishes and sustains for commercial use: the foundation is its steward, with the light obligations of [[CRA Article 24]] ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 28]]).
- A project a company publishes and maintains under its own name without monetising it: the company is its steward, not its manufacturer, even if it uses the project in its own products ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 29]]).
- A project someone monetises: that person is its manufacturer ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 51]]).

Companies that only contribute to or fund a project are not regulated for it ([[CRA Recital 18]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 86]]). For a solo maintainer the person and the project are hard to tell apart, and the CRA's answer is the same either way: no monetisation, no obligations. See [[manufacturers/do-all-projects-have-manufacturers]] and [[stewards/do-all-projects-have-stewards]].
