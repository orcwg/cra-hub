# Will the European Commission provide guidance to help meet the CRA's requirements?

Yes. The Commission has to provide guidance on the CRA, and it has. The CRA requires it to _"publish guidance to assist economic operators in applying this Regulation, with a particular focus on facilitating compliance by microenterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises"_ ([[CRA Article 26(1)]]), covering at least the scope of the regulation, with open source software and remote data processing in particular, support periods, the interplay with other EU law, and substantial modification ([[CRA Article 26(2)]]). The Commission published that guidance on 27 July 2026, as C(2026) 5252 final; its open source part is [[CRA-GUIDANCE Section 3]], and the whole of it is [reproduced on this site][official guidance]. Before it, on 3 December 2025, the Commission published [a set of FAQs on the CRA][commission faq] ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 5]]).

The guidance is the Commission's interpretation: authoritative, but not binding the way the regulation is. Harmonised standards go further, giving a presumption of conformity with the essential requirements they cover ([[CRA Article 27(1)]]; see [[standards/harmonised-standards]]).

ENISA publishes practical material of its own: Technical Advisories on product security, such as on [secure update mechanisms][update advisory] and [software bills of materials][sbom advisory], a [Secure by Design and Default Playbook][playbook], and material on reporting through the single reporting platform ([[srp/faq_17]]). The advisories and the playbook are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence, so a project can reuse and adapt them in its own documentation, with credit and any changes indicated.

The CRA Expert Group is how input from the community reaches the guidance: the Commission has to consult stakeholders when preparing it ([[CRA Article 26(3)]]), and consults the Expert Group as described in [[CRA Article 9]] (see [[cra-itself/cra-expert-group]]). The Commission also has to keep an easy-to-access list of the delegated and implementing acts adopted under the CRA ([[CRA Article 26(2)]]).
