# What does placing a product on the market mean?

Placing on the market is the first time a product with digital elements is supplied in the EU in the course of a commercial activity. The CRA defines _making available on the market_ as _"the supply of a product with digital elements for distribution or use on the Union market in the course of a commercial activity, whether in return for payment or free of charge"_, and _placing on the market_ as _"the first making available"_ ([[CRA Article 3(22)]], [[CRA Article 3(21)]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 10]]). These are the definitions used across EU product law (Blue Guide, sections 2.2 and 2.3). When the manufacturer definition says a manufacturer _markets_ its products, this placing on the market is what it means: see [[manufacturers/what-is-a-manufacturer]]. Placing on the market is the moment from which a product has to comply with the CRA, and the start of the obligations that run from it, such as the support period.

For software, the Commission's guidance settles what "first" means: every copy of a version of standalone software counts as placed on the market at the same time, _"when its manufacturing phase is complete and that software is first supplied for distribution or use on the EU market in the course of a commercial activity"_, _"regardless of when possession or use of each individual copy is transferred"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 14]]). A copy bought two weeks after a release is placed on the market on the release date ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 1]]).

A new version is placed on the market again only if it is a _substantial modification_: a change that affects compliance with the essential requirements or alters the product's intended purpose ([[CRA Article 3(30)]]). An update that is not one, such as a security fix, keeps the original date ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 15]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 2]]). Hardware, and software combined with hardware, follow the Blue Guide's general rules ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 12]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 16]]).

Open source software is placed on the market only when it is monetised: see [[maintainers/monetization]] and [[cra-itself/monetisation]].
