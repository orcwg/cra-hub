# My software embeds an AI model or LLM — is it a product with digital elements?

Generally yes, if it meets the definition of a product with digital elements (see [[scope]]). Embedding a machine-learning model or a large language model does not change the analysis — your software is in scope for the same reasons any connected software is.

In addition, if your product relies on server-side inference that it cannot perform one of its functions without, that back end can itself be in scope as a remote data processing solution under Article 3(2) (see [[remote-processing]]), provided it is developed by, or under the responsibility of, the manufacturer. An inference endpoint that is integral to the product is treated as part of the product, not as a separate, unregulated service.
