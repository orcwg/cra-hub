# My product is also a high-risk AI system under the AI Act — do I need two separate conformity assessments?

In most cases, no — the CRA is designed to avoid duplication.

Under Article 12(1), a product with digital elements that is classified as a high-risk AI system under Article 6 of the AI Act is deemed to comply with the cybersecurity requirements of Article 15 of the AI Act where it meets the CRA essential requirements in Annex I Part I, its processes meet Annex I Part II, and the Article 15 protection level is demonstrated in the EU declaration of conformity issued under the CRA.

Article 12(2) then provides that the AI Act's conformity-assessment procedure (Article 43 of the AI Act) applies — a single assessment — and that AI Act notified bodies may also assess conformity with CRA Annex I.

There is one exception. Under Article 12(3), where the product is also an important product (Annex III) or a critical product (Annex IV), the CRA's own conformity-assessment procedures apply to the cybersecurity requirements, so as to preserve the assurance level (see [[inheriting-product-classification]]).
