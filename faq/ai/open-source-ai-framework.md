# I maintain an open-source AI-agent framework but do not monetise it — am I in scope?

If you make your open-source AI-agent framework available without monetising it, the CRA does not treat that as a commercial activity, and non-monetised free and open-source software is outside the scope of the CRA (Recital 18; see [[monetisation]] and [[profit]]).

If instead you act as an open-source software steward — the role defined in Article 3(14) (see [[what-is-a-steward]]) — you fall under the lighter, tailored regime in Article 24: maintaining a documented, verifiable cybersecurity policy, cooperating with market surveillance authorities, and the reporting duties of Article 14(1), (3) and (8) to the extent of your involvement. Stewards are also exempt from administrative fines under Article 64(10)(b).

The fact that the framework orchestrates AI agents does not change this analysis — the steward question is the same as for any other open-source project.
