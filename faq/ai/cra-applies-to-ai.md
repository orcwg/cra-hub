# Does the CRA apply to AI systems and AI agents?

Yes. There is no AI-specific exception in the CRA. Whether software is regulated is determined by whether it is a product with digital elements (see [[scope]]) and whether its intended purpose or reasonably foreseeable use includes a direct or indirect logical or physical data connection to a device or network (Article 2(1)).

Software that is, or that embeds, an AI system or an autonomous agent is treated like any other software: what matters is the data connection, not the presence of a model. The exclusions in the CRA are sector-specific (see [[out-of-scope]]) and none of them concerns AI as such.
