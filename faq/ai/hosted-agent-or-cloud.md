# Is a hosted AI agent or MCP-style tool server in scope, or is it an out-of-scope cloud service?

It depends on the facts. A hosted component is in scope as a remote data processing solution when it is integral to a product with digital elements and provided under the manufacturer's responsibility (Article 3(2); see [[remote-processing]]).

By contrast, cloud services that are designed and developed outside the responsibility of a manufacturer of a product with digital elements do not fall within the scope of the CRA (Recital 12) — standalone Software-as-a-Service is the example given — and are instead governed by the NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555).

So a hosted AI agent or MCP-style tool server that is an integral part of a product is likely in CRA scope, while one offered as a standalone service developed independently of any product is more likely to sit under NIS2. Where a particular offering falls is fact-specific, and this boundary is one that would benefit from European Commission guidance.
