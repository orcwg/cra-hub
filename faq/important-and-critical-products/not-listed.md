# Your product category is not listed among the important and critical products. Does that mean it is not in scope of the CRA?

No. The lists of important and critical products only pick out the products that face stricter conformity assessment. Most products in scope of the CRA are on neither list, and the CRA applies to them all the same.

Whether a product is in scope is a separate question, which the Commission's FAQ answers: the CRA applies to _"products with digital elements made available on the market, the intended purpose or reasonably foreseeable use of which includes a direct or indirect logical or physical data connection to a device or network"_, unless an exemption applies ([[official/faq_1-1]], [[CRA Article 2(1)]]). For what that covers, see [[cra-itself/scope]] and [[cra-itself/out-of-scope]].

The lists serve another purpose. A product whose core functionality matches a category in [[CRA Annex III]] is an _important product_, and one matching [[CRA Annex IV]] a _critical product_ ([[CRA Article 7(1)]], [[CRA Article 8(1)]]); the Commission's FAQ points to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 for the technical description of each category ([[official/faq_3-1]]). The Commission's guidance says this classification _"is of relevance when determining the conformity assessment procedure the manufacturer needs to follow"_, and that products in the default category _"can always rely on the internal control procedure based on module A ('self-assessment')"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 137]]). The essential requirements of [[CRA Annex I]] are the same for every product in scope.

So if your product is in scope but in no listed category, it is in the default category: you still have to meet the essential requirements, handle vulnerabilities, report and draw up the technical documentation, but you can assess conformity yourself. For the difference that makes, see [[manufacturers/self-assessment]]; for what happens when a product matches several categories, see [[important-and-critical-products/multiple-categories]].
