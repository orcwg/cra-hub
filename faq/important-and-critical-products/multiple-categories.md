# If my product has features from several important or critical product categories, which requirements apply?

The same ones as for any product: the essential cybersecurity requirements of [[CRA Annex I]], once, for the product as a whole. The CRA has no requirements per product category. The categories of important and critical products in [[CRA Annex III]] and [[CRA Annex IV]] decide which conformity assessment procedure the manufacturer has to follow, not which requirements apply ([[official/faq_3-1]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 137]]), and the "vertical" harmonised standards being written for those categories are one way to show conformity, not extra requirements ([[official/faq_6-10]]).

Which requirements of Annex I are relevant, and how far, follows from the product's cybersecurity risk assessment, which has to cover all of the product's features, whether or not it is an important or critical product ([[official/faq_3-3]], [[official/faq_4-1-3]]). The Commission's guidance says the manufacturer _"needs to ensure that the product with digital elements as a whole meets the essential requirements, considering, as appropriate, the security of the components or functionalities integrated into it"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 147]]). Where several harmonised standards cover different parts of the product, each gives a presumption of conformity for the requirements it covers ([[CRA Article 27(1)]]), and the rest is for the manufacturer to show.

Having features of several categories rarely puts a product in several categories. What counts is the product's _core functionality_, the main features without which it cannot meet its intended purpose; extra functions, including components that are themselves important or critical products, do not change it ([[official/faq_3-4]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 139]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 140]]). A smartphone with an operating system and a password manager has the core functionality of neither ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 58]]). Neither the CRA nor the Commission's guidance says which procedure applies to a product that has the core functionality of more than one category; see [[important-and-critical-products/inheriting-product-classification]] for integrated components.
