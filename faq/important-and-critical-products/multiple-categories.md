# If your product has features from several important or critical product categories, which requirements apply?

The same ones as for any product: the essential cybersecurity requirements of [[CRA Annex I]], once, for the product as a whole. The CRA has no requirements per product category. The categories in [[CRA Annex III]] and [[CRA Annex IV]] decide which conformity assessment procedure applies, not which requirements ([[official/faq_3-1]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 137]]), and the "vertical" harmonised standards written for them are one way to show conformity, not extra requirements ([[official/faq_6-10]]).

Which requirements of Annex I are relevant follows from the product's risk assessment, which has to cover all of its features ([[official/faq_3-3]], [[official/faq_4-1-3]]): the manufacturer _"needs to ensure that the product with digital elements as a whole meets the essential requirements, considering, as appropriate, the security of the components or functionalities integrated into it"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 147]]). Where several harmonised standards cover different parts of the product, each gives a presumption of conformity for what it covers ([[CRA Article 27(1)]]); the rest is for the manufacturer to show.

A product never belongs to several categories: it _"may not have more than one core functionality for the purposes of determining the applicable conformity assessment regime"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 144]]). The category follows from the product's _core functionality_, the main features without which it cannot meet its intended purpose; extra functions, including components that are themselves important or critical products, do not change it ([[official/faq_3-4]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 139]], [[CRA-GUIDANCE point 152]]). An antivirus with disk-cleaning and anti-tracking functions follows the antivirus category's procedure, applies the harmonised standard for its core functionality and adds its own measures for the rest ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 62]]). Only where the modules are sold separately is each its own product, classified on its own ([[CRA-GUIDANCE Example 61]]). For integrated components, see [[important-and-critical-products/inheriting-product-classification]].
