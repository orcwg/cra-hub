# What qualifies a product as an important or critical product?

A product is an important or critical product when its core functionality is that of a product category the CRA lists: a category in [[CRA Annex III]] makes it an _important product_, in class I or II, and a category in [[CRA Annex IV]] a _critical product_ ([[CRA Article 7(1)]], [[CRA Article 8(1)]]). Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 gives the technical description of each category ([[official/faq_3-1]], [[CRA-CATEGORIES Annex I]], [[CRA-CATEGORIES Annex II]]). A product in no listed category is in the default category, as most products in scope are: see [[important-and-critical-products/not-listed]].

A product's core functionality is _"its main features and technical capabilities, without which it would not be able to meet its intended purpose"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 139]]). Extra features don't take a product out of a category, and containing a listed product, such as an operating system in a smartphone, doesn't put it in one ([[official/faq_3-4]], [[CRA Article 7(1)]]).

The lists are exhaustive. The categories are products that primarily perform functions critical to the cybersecurity of other products, networks or services, or whose functions carry a significant risk of adverse effects on many other products or on the health, security or safety of users ([[CRA Article 7(2)]]). Those criteria guide the Commission, which alone can add, move or withdraw a category, by delegated act ([[CRA Article 7(3)]]); a manufacturer doesn't apply them to its own product.

The category only decides the conformity assessment procedure, not the security requirements: see [[manufacturers/self-assessment]] and [[important-and-critical-products/multiple-categories]].
