# What qualifies a product as an important or critical product?

Being in one of the product categories the CRA lists: a product whose core functionality matches a category in [[CRA Annex III]] is an _important product_, in class I or II, and one matching a category in [[CRA Annex IV]] is a _critical product_ ([[CRA Article 7(1)]], [[CRA Article 8(1)]]). The lists are exhaustive. A product in no listed category is in the default category, as most products in scope are: see [[important-and-critical-products/not-listed]].

The Commission's FAQ says _"a manufacturer should look at the core functionality of its product with digital elements to determine whether that product is an important or critical product"_, and points to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 for the technical description of each category ([[official/faq_3-1]], [[CRA-CATEGORIES Annex I]], [[CRA-CATEGORIES Annex II]]). The Commission's guidance defines core functionality as _"that product's main features and technical capabilities, without which it would not be able to meet its intended purpose"_ ([[CRA-GUIDANCE point 139]]). Extra features do not take a product out of a category, and integrating a listed product, such as an operating system in a smartphone, does not put the whole product in one ([[official/faq_3-4]]).

The CRA does give criteria: the listed categories are products that primarily perform functions critical to the cybersecurity of other products, networks or services, or whose functions carry a significant risk of adverse effects on many other products or on the health, security or safety of users ([[CRA Article 7(2)]]). These are the grounds on which the Commission chose the categories and may change the list by delegated act ([[CRA Article 7(3)]]), not a test a manufacturer applies to its own product.

Classification only decides the conformity assessment procedure, not the requirements: see [[manufacturers/self-assessment]] and [[important-and-critical-products/multiple-categories]]. ORC WG's comments on the Commission's technical descriptions are in [its input to the implementing regulation](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/product-definitions/input-to-draft-regulation.md).
